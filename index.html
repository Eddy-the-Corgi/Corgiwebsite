Interests

I am mainly interested in the analysis of deep neural networks, such as large-width limits. Essentially, we send various parameters to infinity and see what happens. It's pretty much analogous to obtaining a "Law of Large Numbers", but for mathematical descriptions of various familiar objects such as neural networks.
It's essentially a cross of analysis (which I strongly resonate with and find relatively intuitive) and machine learning (which provides concrete objects that are relevant for applications).
Some of my auxiliary interests include the mathematisation of LLMs and problems such as AI alignment, and scientific modelling. To that end, I'm working on PDEs and Probability Theory, and soon SDEs and SPDEs. ^^
I've previously worked on projects involving stochastic block models and PDE diffusion models.
Trying to improve on my algebra, and occasionally read about random stuff like social theories. :3
